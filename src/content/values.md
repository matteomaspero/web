## How I work

### Openness
I try to be explicit about what a project is for, where it is going, and what I expect — early, in writing, and to everyone involved. The same instinct makes me publish code and data where I can. Being open about expectations is awkward for a week and saves a year.

### Thoroughness, in proportion
I like doing things properly. I also think knowing *how much* rigour a question deserves is part of doing it properly — a clinical release document and a Tuesday email are not the same object, and reporting more digits than the measurement supports is its own kind of sloppiness.

### Lightness
Calvino put *leggerezza* first among the qualities worth carrying into this century, and meant it as a way of carrying weight, not avoiding it — Perseus looking at Medusa through the shield rather than head-on. In oncology there is no shortage of weight. Humour, and a willingness to be the joke myself, isn't a distraction from serious work; it's part of what makes serious work sustainable.
